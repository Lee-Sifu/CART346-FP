# Five phases

## Phase 1: entering/waking in a cave 
1 bgm from Haolei
1 effect from Jason
1 sound/effect from Jordan

## Phase 2: panic mode: 
- afraid of the fact that you are not alone
- their might be some goblins ready to attack
- or the player hears the boss nearby 
entering a room with hostile mobs 
1 bgm from Haolei
1 effect from Jason
1 sound/effect from Jordan

## Phase 3: running away
- the boss found you and is coming after you
- getting stuck and trying their hardest to escape
Escaping the boss

## Phase 4: return to boss after finding a loot chest
- after the player narrowly escapes the boss, they hear an angelic hum
- where they find a weapon to use against the boss
- they return to fight off the boss 

## Phase 5: victory/escaping the cave
- after defeating the boss the player hears either
- a water fall followed by forest noises
- a crowd of people in a village
- or both
  and the player returns to civilization after the things they experienced
